How it works

A plain-English walkthrough of one prediction.

Each game is predicted as a point margin from five things: season-long team strength (a margin-of-victory Elo rating), recent form (a recency-weighted net rating per 100 possessions), home court, rest (days off, back-to-backs, three games in four nights), and injuries & availability (players missing from each team's rotation compared with its recent norm, from box scores and the ESPN injury report). The margin becomes a win probability using how much NBA results vary from game to game.

The rest of the league schedule is simulated 10,000 times. Each simulation shifts every team's strength by a random amount reflecting how uncertain we are about it, then plays out every game, applies tiebreakers (head-to-head, division winner, conference record), and runs the play-in.

The model is trained on every regular-season game since 2015-16 and tested walk-forward: each test season is predicted using only data from before it. On top of that, the live injury report adjusts each game: players listed out or day-to-day reduce the team's strength by how much they usually contribute.
